============================================================
  HOW CASINOS MAKE ROULETTE UNFAIR — THE ZERO TRICK
============================================================

A fair roulette wheel has 36 pockets: numbers 1 through 36,
half red and half black. If that's all there was, the game
would be perfectly fair — the casino earns nothing long-term.

So casinos add zeros. That's the entire trick.

  European Roulette → adds one zero  (0)   → 37 total pockets
  American Roulette → adds two zeros (0,00) → 38 total pockets

------------------------------------------------------------
HOW THE MATH CHANGES
------------------------------------------------------------

When you bet on a single number, the payout is always 35 to 1.
That payout was designed for a 36-pocket wheel.

On a FAIR wheel (36 pockets):
  P(win) = 1/36
  Expected return = (35 × 1/36) + (-1 × 35/36)
                  = 35/36 - 35/36 = 0%
  The game is exactly fair. Neither side has an edge.

On a EUROPEAN wheel (37 pockets):
  P(win) = 1/37
  Expected return = (35 × 1/37) + (-1 × 36/37)
                  = 35/37 - 36/37 = -1/37 ≈ -2.70%
  The casino takes 2.70 cents of every dollar bet, on average.

On an AMERICAN wheel (38 pockets):
  P(win) = 1/38
  Expected return = (35 × 1/38) + (-1 × 37/38)
                  = 35/38 - 37/38 = -2/38 ≈ -5.26%
  The casino takes 5.26 cents of every dollar bet, on average.

This percentage is called the HOUSE EDGE.

------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE SIMULATION CONFIRMS
------------------------------------------------------------

The code in 02.Change-in-code-UNFAIR.py runs 20 trials at
increasing spin counts (1,000 → 10,000 → 100,000 → 1,000,000)
for all three wheel types and prints the average expected return.

Results (from the simulation):

  Spins      Fair Roulette    European    American
  -------------------------------------------------
  1,000       +6.56%          -2.26%      -8.92%
  10,000      -1.23%          -4.17%      -5.75%
  100,000     +0.81%          -2.65%      -5.11%
  1,000,000   -0.07%          -2.73%      -5.21%

Notice what happens as spins increase:

  Fair Roulette → converges to 0%       (no house edge)
  European      → converges to -2.70%   (1/37 house edge)
  American      → converges to -5.26%   (2/38 house edge)

With only 1,000 spins there's a lot of noise — variance is high.
By 1,000,000 spins the Law of Large Numbers has kicked in fully
and the true house edge is clearly visible.

------------------------------------------------------------
WHY YOU CANNOT BEAT THE CASINO
------------------------------------------------------------

Every betting system ever invented — Martingale, Fibonacci,
D'Alembert, flat betting — all fail for the same reason:

  They change WHEN you win and lose.
  They cannot change the expected return per spin.

If every spin has a -2.70% expected return, then over N spins
your total expected return is also -2.70%, no matter how you
arrange your bets. The math is baked in at the wheel level.

The only way to have a positive expected return at roulette
is to use a biased wheel — one that physically lands on
certain numbers more often. This does happen in practice
(worn wheels, manufacturing defects), but casinos monitor
for it carefully.

------------------------------------------------------------
THE ZERO IN PRACTICE
------------------------------------------------------------

When the ball lands on 0 (or 00):
  - All outside bets (red/black, odd/even, high/low) LOSE.
  - The casino collects everything.
  - The zero pockets don't pay out at 35:1 unless you
    specifically bet on them — which most players don't.

That single extra pocket, representing roughly 2.7% of the
wheel, is where all of the casino's profit comes from.
