============================================================
  HOW CASINOS MAKE ROULETTE UNFAIR — THE ZERO
============================================================

A fair roulette wheel contains 36 pockets: numbers 1 through 36,
evenly divided between red and black. On such a wheel, the game
is mathematically fair — the casino holds no long-term advantage.

Casinos solve this problem by adding zero pockets.

  European Roulette → one zero  (0)    → 37 total pockets
  American Roulette → two zeros (0, 00) → 38 total pockets

The payout structure remains unchanged. That asymmetry is the
entire source of the house's edge.

------------------------------------------------------------
THE MATHEMATICS OF THE HOUSE EDGE
------------------------------------------------------------

A single-number bet pays 35 to 1 on every variant of the wheel.
This payout was designed for 36 pockets — it is exactly fair
on a 36-pocket wheel and becomes progressively worse for the
player as pockets are added.

On a FAIR wheel (36 pockets):
  P(win) = 1/36
  Expected return = (35 × 1/36) + (−1 × 35/36)
                  = 35/36 − 35/36 = 0%
  Neither side has an edge.

On a EUROPEAN wheel (37 pockets):
  P(win) = 1/37
  Expected return = (35 × 1/37) + (−1 × 36/37)
                  = 35/37 − 36/37 = −1/37 ≈ −2.70%
  The casino extracts 2.70 cents per dollar wagered, on average.

On an AMERICAN wheel (38 pockets):
  P(win) = 1/38
  Expected return = (35 × 1/38) + (−1 × 37/38)
                  = 35/38 − 37/38 = −2/38 ≈ −5.26%
  The casino extracts 5.26 cents per dollar wagered, on average.

This figure — the casino's average take per unit wagered — is
called the HOUSE EDGE.

------------------------------------------------------------
SIMULATION RESULTS
------------------------------------------------------------

The simulation in 02.Change-in-code-UNFAIR.py runs 20 independent
trials across increasing spin counts for all three wheel types,
recording the average expected return at each scale.

  Spins        Fair Roulette    European    American
  ---------------------------------------------------
  1,000         +6.56%          −2.26%      −8.92%
  10,000        −1.23%          −4.17%      −5.75%
  100,000       +0.81%          −2.65%      −5.11%
  1,000,000     −0.07%          −2.73%      −5.21%

At 1,000 spins, variance is high and the results are noisy —
the Fair Roulette return of +6.56% appears profitable, and the
American wheel at −8.92% appears far worse than theory predicts.
Neither observation is meaningful at this sample size.

By 1,000,000 spins, the Law of Large Numbers has fully asserted
itself. The estimates converge tightly to their theoretical values:

  Fair Roulette → 0%       (theoretical: 0%)
  European      → −2.70%  (theoretical: −1/37 ≈ −2.703%)
  American      → −5.26%  (theoretical: −2/38 ≈ −5.263%)

The simulation is accurately recovering the analytically derived
house edge values. This is Monte Carlo working exactly as intended.

------------------------------------------------------------
WHY NO BETTING SYSTEM CAN OVERCOME THE HOUSE EDGE
------------------------------------------------------------

Every betting system ever proposed — Martingale, Fibonacci,
D'Alembert, flat betting — fails for the same fundamental reason:

  Betting systems alter the sequence of wagers.
  They do not alter the expected return per spin.

If every spin carries an expected return of −2.70%, then over
N spins the cumulative expected return is also −2.70%, regardless
of how bets are sized or sequenced. The edge is not a function
of bet size — it is a structural property of the wheel itself.

The only scenario in which a player can achieve a positive
expected return is a physically biased wheel — one that lands on
certain numbers with above-average frequency due to mechanical
wear or manufacturing imperfection. Casinos monitor wheel
performance precisely to detect and eliminate this possibility.

------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT HAPPENS WHEN THE BALL LANDS ON ZERO
------------------------------------------------------------

When the outcome is 0 or 00:
  - All outside bets (red/black, odd/even, 1-18/19-36) lose.
  - The casino collects the full stake on every such bet.
  - The zero pockets pay 35:1 only if directly bet upon,
    which the majority of players never do.

That single additional pocket — approximately 2.7% of the
wheel in the European variant — accounts for the entirety of
the casino's mathematical advantage in roulette.
