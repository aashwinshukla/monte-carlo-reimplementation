      - Count total points and points inside circle

MATH REMINDERS
------------------------------------------------------------

π estimation:
  A unit square has area 1.
  A quarter-circle of radius 1 has area π/4.
  So: π ≈ 4 × (points inside quarter-circle / total points)

Quarter-circle condition: x² + y² ≤ 1  (x,y ∈ [0,1])

Dice theoretical probabilities:
  Sum 2  → 1/36 ≈ 2.78%    Sum 7  → 6/36 ≈ 16.67%
  Sum 3  → 2/36 ≈ 5.56%    Sum 8  → 5/36 ≈ 13.89%
  Sum 4  → 3/36 ≈ 8.33%    Sum 9  → 4/36 ≈ 11.11%
  Sum 5  → 4/36 ≈ 11.11%   Sum 10 → 3/36 ≈  8.33%
  Sum 6  → 5/36 ≈ 13.89%   Sum 11 → 2/36 ≈  5.56%
                             Sum 12 → 1/36 ≈  2.78%
